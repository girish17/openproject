**Announcing Yojana — Open-Source Portfolio & Project Management, Powered by AI** 🚀

Enterprise portfolio tools like Jira Align and IBM Aptio cost a fortune, lock you in, and ship your data to third-party AI APIs. There hasn't been a serious open-source alternative — until now.

I built **Yojana** to change that.

**What is Yojana?**
A free, open-source platform that blends enterprise-grade portfolio management with local-first AI. No per-seat licensing. No vendor lock-in. No data leaving your infrastructure.

**What it does:**
- 📊 **Portfolio Management** — Roadmaps, cross-project visibility, resource perspective, strategic alignment. The kind of tool that connects daily execution to portfolio decisions.
- 🤖 **AI Chat & Agents** — Ask questions across your portfolio, create and update work packages, automate routine tasks — all in natural language, running on your own Ollama instance.
- 🔒 **Private by default** — Local LLM inference. Choose any open model (Llama 3, Mistral, etc.). Zero data ever leaves your network.
- 🏗️ **Built on OpenProject** — Enterprise foundations (Gantt, Agile boards, time tracking, BCF for BIM) with a modern AI-native layer on top.

**Stack:** Ruby on Rails + Hotwire + TypeScript. Ships as a single Docker image, deploy anywhere.

Jira Align and Aptio charge per-user, per-year, per-feature. Yojana is GPLv3-licensed (same as upstream), self-hosted, and built for teams that want control over their data and their roadmap.
