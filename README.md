# yojanā

![yojana logo](app/assets/images/logo_yojana.png)

yojanā is a web-based project management software. Use yojanā to manage your portfolios, projects, tasks and goals. 


yojanā's key features are:

* Project planning and scheduling
* Portfolio management
* Kanban Boards
* Product roadmap and release planning
* Task management and team collaboration
* Agile and Scrum
* Time tracking, cost reporting, and budgeting
* Bug tracking
* Meeting agendas and meeting minutes

Currently, portfolio management is widely used through proprietary products such as Jira Align, IBM Aptio etc. The license and subscription for these products is quite expensive and there's no equivalent quality alternative available in the free/open source market. Yojanā addresses this problem and offers a free and open source alternative to those proprietary products. There is no vendor lock-in or need for any paid subscription in yojanā to use its features as it is quite essential for small enterprises, startups and teams.
